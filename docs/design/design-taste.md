## Type
- Tentative: likes Montserrat SemiBold, lowercase wordmarks (from the reference image).
- Tentative: wants an "industry-level" face rather than a basic one.

## Layout & density
- Tentative: prefers few elements at once; reacts strongly to overwhelming screens.

## Motion
- Tentative: wants simple animation, user-friendly.

## Concept / metaphor
- Tentative: wants creativity and a clear concept, not flat "safe" corporate.

- Keeps metrics (money totals) on their own tool, not on the home screen.
- Copy: plain English only, no Taglish.
