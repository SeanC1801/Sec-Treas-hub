# Review questions: answer these before any work

Router: [agents.md](../00_CORE/agents.md). Back to: [overview.md](overview.md). Rules: [rules.md](../00_CORE/rules.md). Then: [treasurer.md](../01_SPECS/treasurer.md) (Nico) or [secretary.md](../01_SPECS/secretary.md) (Ian).

How the AI asks:

- One question at a time. Wait for the answer.
- Short, simple words. Every question comes with an example answer after "Like:".
- If the person is unsure, give another example or explain in simple words.
- Write down every flaw and error they list. They become tasks at the end.

## Step 1. Who you are

1. "Are you Nico (Treasurer) or Ian (Secretary)?"
   - If the answer is anyone else: "This handoff is only for Nico and Ian. Please talk to Sean." Then stop.

## Step 2. Open the hub

1. "Please open the live hub: https://sec-treas-hub.vercel.app/. Open it on your computer (a wide window) and on your phone. Tell me when both are open. Like: 'Both are open.'"
2. "Choose Test Officer and press Continue with Google. Then switch to [Treasurer / Secretary] at the top of the Events page. Tell me when you see your side. Like: 'I see the Treasurer side.'"

## Step 3. Your section of the hub

Go through the person's own screens, one at a time:

- Nico (Treasurer): Events (grid and list), an event's page, Budget Tracker, a Budget Request (Details, Program flow, Budget, Preview & print), a Liquidation Report (Request, Receipts, Proof, Preview & print).
- Ian (Secretary): Events (grid and list), an event's page, Minutes (Details, Notes, Action items, Preview & print), Certificates (Details, Names, Preview & print).

For each screen, ask on the computer first, then on the phone.

### On the computer

1. "Is any button hidden, covered, or hard to press? Like: 'the Continue bar covers the names in Minutes.'"
2. "Does any button do nothing, or the wrong thing? Like: 'Print opens a blank page.'"
3. "When you scroll, does anything move out of place, overlap, or get cut off? Like: 'the bottom bar floats in the middle.'"
4. "Is any text wrong or confusing? Like: 'it says Receipts but it should say Expenses.'"
5. "Did you see an error? Like: 'a red message popped up', or red lines in the console (press F12, then Console)."

### On the phone

1. "Is any button hidden, covered, or too small to tap? Like: 'the Open button in Budget Tracker is too small.'"
2. "Does anything run off the side of the screen? Like: 'I have to scroll sideways on the Receipts step.'"
3. "Does the menu button (top left) open and close the sidebar? Like: 'yes, it works.'"
4. "When the keyboard is open, can you still see what you are typing? Like: 'the keyboard covers the amount field.'"
5. "Does anything feel slow or jumpy? Like: 'scrolling Events stutters.'"

## Step 4. List your flaws and errors

1. "Now list every flaw or error you found in your section, one per line. Like:
   - Desktop, Minutes, Details: the Continue bar covers the attendee names.
   - Phone, Budget Tracker: the Open button is too small."
2. "Did I miss anything? Like: 'add that Print is slow on my phone.'"

## Step 5. Your work as an officer

1. "How do you make your documents today? Which step is slow or missing in the hub? Like: 'I also need to attach the CSC approval slip.'"
2. "Does any document look different from the official template? Like: 'the signature lines are in the wrong order.'"
3. "Is there anything in the hub you would remove? Like: 'I never use the Program flow step.'"

## Step 6. Your templates

The hub makes documents from templates. Find out which ones they use, so the hub matches them.

1. "Do you have any documents you use as a template, like an official form or a past document you copy from? Like: 'yes, the CSC Budget Request form in our Drive' or 'no.'"
2. For each one, ask:
   - "What is it called, and which document does it make? Like: 'CSC Liquidation Report form, for liquidations.'"
   - "Where is it? Like: 'a Google Drive link', 'a .docx on my laptop', or 'a printed copy.'"
   - "Is it the newest version from your senior or the org? Like: 'yes, Alexis gave it to me in October.'"
   - "Is anything in it different from what the hub prints? Like: 'the signature lines are in a different order.'"
3. Compare it with the templates the hub already follows (listed under "My templates" in their task file). Ask: "Is the hub using the same template as you, or a different one? Like: 'same one' or 'mine is newer.'"
4. Write each template under "My templates" in their task file, following [rules.md](../00_CORE/rules.md), part 9. Any difference becomes a new task.
5. Remind them: "Before adding a template file to the code, remove real names, amounts and signatures, and ask your senior if it is the right version."

## Step 7. Your own additions

1. "Would you like to add anything to the hub? Like: 'a button to duplicate last month's minutes.'"
2. "Would you like to change or remove any task in your task file? Like: 'skip T4 for now.'"

## Step 8. Your guardrails

1. "Would you like to add guardrails to your work? These are rules I will always follow. Like: 'never delete a file', 'only touch my own files', 'show me every change before saving it'. You can say no."
2. Write the answers under "My guardrails" in the person's task file. More examples are in [rules.md](../00_CORE/rules.md), part 6.

## Step 9. Confirm the list

1. Write each flaw, error or idea as a new task under "Your own tasks" in [treasurer.md](../01_SPECS/treasurer.md) (T5, T6, ...) or [secretary.md](../01_SPECS/secretary.md) (S5, S6, ...), with:
   - what they saw or want,
   - computer, phone, or both,
   - the screen,
   - steps to see it, if it is a bug.
2. Show the full list and ask: "Is this list right? Would you like to add anything else? Like: 'yes, that's all' or 'add one more.'"
3. Ask: "Which task do you want to do first? Like: 'T1.'"
4. Remind them: "If this task changes what a document says or looks like, ask your senior first (Alexis for Nico, Trizy for Ian). Would you like me to write that message for you?"
5. Only then start the task, following [rules.md](../00_CORE/rules.md), part 4.
